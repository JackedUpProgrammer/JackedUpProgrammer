## Mzansi Work

> **Overlooked people. Overlooked opportunities.**

Most South African jobs never reach LinkedIn or Indeed. The retail floor manager, the learnership candidate, the apprentice electrician, the call-centre agent keeping the country running — their vacancies live on municipal PDFs, forgotten career portals and WhatsApp groups.

**Mzansi Work** is a low-data job discovery and application-tracking platform built for entry-level, first-time and overlooked South African job seekers. Real vacancies, from real employers, surfaced where people can actually find them.
